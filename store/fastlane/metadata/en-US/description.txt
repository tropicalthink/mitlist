The shared household, all in one place. Split expenses, rotate chores, manage shopping lists, and plan meals — free and open source. No ads, no subscriptions, no lock-in.

WHY MITLIST
You're already paying rent. Why pay another subscription for apps that do less? mitlist is free and open source. Host it yourself in 5 minutes with Docker.

CORE FEATURES
• Expense splitting — Equal, exact, %, or shares with settlement tracking
• Chore rotation — Round-robin, fixed, or cron with subtasks and load tracking
• Shopping lists — Shared with item claiming, cost tracking, expense generation
• Meal plans + recipes — Weekly planner, AI recipe clipping, auto-generate lists
• Unified calendar — Chores, meals, expenses, and reminders in one view
• Pinwall — Corkboard notices and household announcements
• Offline-first — Works without internet, syncs when you're back online
• Scanner — OCR recipes, receipts, and lists from photos
• Data export — Download your expenses as CSV or JSON anytime
• Multi-household — Switch between groups, one account

YOUR DATA, YOUR RULES
No account required on self-hosted instances. Export your data anytime. No telemetry, no tracking, no ads. Open source (AGPL-3.0).

PRICE
Free. No in-app purchases. No subscriptions.
