mitlist is the shared household coordination app that puts you in control. Split expenses, rotate chores, manage shopping lists, and plan meals — all in one place, with an optional Premium subscription for larger households.

WHY MITLIST
You're already paying rent. Why pay another subscription for apps that do less? mitlist is free and open source. Host it yourself in 5 minutes with Docker.

CORE FEATURES
• Expense splitting — Equal, exact, %, or shares. Settlement tracking with reimbursement suggestions. Recurring expenses handled automatically.
• Chore rotation — Round-robin, fixed, or cron schedules. Subtasks, skip, undo, reschedule. Load tracking to see who's pulling their weight.
• Shopping lists — Shared lists with item claiming, cost tracking, and one-tap expense generation.
• Meal plans + recipes — Weekly planner, recipe clipping via AI, auto-generate shopping lists from meal plans.
• Calendar — Unified view of chores, meals, expenses, and reminders.
• Pinwall — Household notices, reminders, and announcements.
• Offline-first — Works without internet. Syncs when you're back online.
• Scanner — OCR recipes, receipts, and lists from photos.
• Multi-household — Switch between groups. One account, many households.

YOUR DATA, YOUR RULES
• No account required on self-hosted instances
• Export expenses as CSV or JSON anytime
• No telemetry, no usage tracking
• Open source (AGPL-3.0) — auditable, transparent

PRICE
Free for households of up to four people. Optional monthly or yearly Premium unlocks larger households and can be managed or canceled through Google Play. No ads.
