mitlist is the shared household coordination app that puts you in control. Split expenses, rotate chores, manage shopping lists, and plan meals — all in one place, with an optional Premium subscription for larger households.

WHY MITLIST
You're already paying rent. Why pay another subscription for apps that do less? mitlist is free and open source. Host it yourself in 5 minutes with Docker, or use our hosted platform — your data stays yours.

FEATURES
- Expense splitting: Equal, exact amounts, percentages, or shares. Track who owes whom with automatic settlement suggestions. Recurring expenses handled automatically.
- Chore rotation: Round-robin, fixed assignment, or custom cron schedules. Subtasks, skip reasons, undo, and reschedule. See who's doing their fair share with load tracking.
- Shopping lists: Shared lists with item claiming, cost tracking, and one-tap expense generation when you're done shopping.
- Meal plans + recipes: Weekly planner with breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack slots. Clip recipes from any URL, scan from photos, or create manually. Auto-generate shopping lists from your meal plan.
- Calendar: Unified view of chores, meal plans, expenses, and reminders. Everything your household needs in one timeline.
- Pinwall: Corkboard-style notices, reminders, and household announcements. Link posts to chores, lists, or expenses.
- Offline-first: Works without internet. Shopping lists and chores sync when you're back online.
- Scanner: OCR recipes, receipts, and lists from photos using AI.
- Multi-household: Switch between groups. One account, many households.

YOUR DATA, YOUR RULES
- No account required on self-hosted instances — connect the app directly to your server
- Export expenses as CSV or JSON anytime from the app
- Delete your account and your data is gone. Export first if you want it.
- No telemetry, no usage tracking, no analytics on self-hosted instances

OPEN SOURCE
mitlist is AGPL-3.0 licensed. The code is public and auditable. No hidden fees, no data mining, no dark patterns. Just a tool for households to stay organized.

PRICE
Free for households of up to four people. Optional monthly or yearly Premium unlocks larger households and can be managed or canceled through your App Store account. No ads.
