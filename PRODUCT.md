# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Flatmates and household members sharing a living space. They coordinate everyday domestic life together: splitting shopping lists, tracking shared expenses, dividing chores, and managing household meals and calendar. They use the app throughout the day in short bursts — checking what's due, marking things done, adding an item to the shopping list mid-errand.

## Product Purpose

Shared household coordination for lists, money, chores, and recipes. mitlist reduces the friction of living with other people: who owes what, what needs doing, what to buy. Success looks like a household that runs without anyone having to chase anyone else.

## Brand Personality

Direct, warm, practical. No corporate polish, no gamification. The interface has opinions (hard edges, flat shadows, a strong primary color) but never gets in the way of the task. It feels like a shared notebook that works.

## Anti-references

- **Generic Material Design**: Default blue-and-white Android look, no personality, indistinguishable from a thousand other apps.
- **Notion / Obsidian**: Too tool-like and monochromatic. mitlist is for people, not power users. It has warmth.
- **Pastel lifestyle apps**: Soft gradients, rounded corners, Instagram-aesthetic productivity. Splitwise's softness, Any.do's prettiness. mitlist should feel grounded, not decorative.

## Design Principles

1. **Disappear into the task.** The UI clears the path. One primary action per screen, states are obvious, nothing competes for attention.
2. **Commit, don't hedge.** Hard edges, offset shadows, a strong orange. Every visual decision is made with conviction. No "soft" fallbacks.
3. **Warmth without decoration.** The household context is personal. Warmth is expressed through copy, empty states, and the color system — not gradients or rounded corners.
4. **What's due, clearly.** Household coordination lives and dies on seeing what needs attention. Overdue chores, outstanding balances, and unpurchased list items should be immediately scannable.
5. **Short sessions, reliable state.** Users open the app mid-errand or while cooking. State persists, offline works, interactions are snappy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. DisableAnimations (reduced motion) is already supported in the animation system. All interactive elements should be keyboard-reachable and screen-reader labeled. Touch targets minimum 44×44pt.
