# Commons handwriting samples

Real handwritten shopping lists from Wikimedia Commons, used only as an OCR
evaluation set (never for training). Each file keeps its original license;
images are the 1600 px renditions Commons serves.

Ground truth in `frontend/test/fixtures/ocr_eval_commons_manifest.json` was
transcribed from the photos by an AI assistant (Claude) and should be checked
by a person. Lines it could not read with confidence are marked illegible
rather than guessed.
